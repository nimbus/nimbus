# Technical Debt

Last updated: 2026-06-07

This ledger tracks known, actionable Nimbus debt that is not better owned by a
single active execution plan. It is intentionally cross-cutting: when a plan
discovers a durable gap, record it here instead of leaving it hidden in a
comment, archived plan, or conversation.

## Categories

- **Fidelity**: Behavior that differs from the service or protocol Nimbus
  emulates.
- **Cleanup**: Maintainability or code-quality work that reduces future
  confusion.
- **Security**: Security, auth, policy, isolation, or injection-hardening work.
- **Testing**: Missing or inadequate tests.
- **Architecture**: Ownership, boundary, or invariant work.
- **Performance**: Latency, throughput, allocation, or scaling work.
- **Observability**: Metrics, tracing, proof, or diagnostic gaps.
